Description

Ikigai is a book which talks about finding your purpose and then following it with all heart to live a happy and satisfying life. The authors take us on this gentle search along with them and make us understand how the long-lived centenarians of Okinawa, Japan enjoy continuous flow, resilient longevity, and the blissful art of daily mindful fulfillment.
